Owner / Branch Manager

Run the workshop like an OS

See revenue, OpEx, bay load, and open jobs at a glance. Drill into GST summaries and technician productivity without leaving the dashboard.

  1. Morning pulseReview KPIs: jobs today, revenue MTD, low stock, pending approvals.
  2. Sales vs OpExCompare six-month sales trends against rent, utilities, and payroll expense categories.
  3. Bay & staff loadSpot bottlenecks before the afternoon rush; reassign work across technicians.
  4. GST healthMonth-to-date taxable value, CGST/SGST/IGST split, and invoice counts.

Service Advisor

From walk-in to approved estimate

Create job cards, capture inspection findings, draft GST-aware estimates, and push WhatsApp approvals before technicians start.

  1. Check-inMatch booking or create walk-in; verify registration, odometer, and PUC.
  2. InspectMark checklist items Passed / Attention / Critical with notes and photos.
  3. EstimateAdd labour + parts with HSN/SAC; system computes CGST/SGST or IGST.
  4. WhatsApp approveCustomer receives the quote, approves lines, and work can begin.
